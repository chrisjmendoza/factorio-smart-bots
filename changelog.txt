Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-08
  Features:
    - Roboports have 8 charging pads instead of 4, laid out as an even ring, and charge at twice the power.
    - Robots carry a larger battery and spend less energy flying, so they need fewer charges to begin with.
    - A roboport whose pads are all busy lends its buffered energy directly to the robots queued behind them.
    - A congested roboport that reaches its energy reserve can borrow from the roboports it connects to.
    - Optional overlay showing how many robots are waiting to charge above each congested roboport.
    - Every number is a mod setting; the lending behaviour can be switched off to leave charging to the base game.
